Change Tracking is a lightweight solution that provides an efficient data change tracking

mechanism for applications, ETL processes, event capture, and auditing. This allows for quick

and basic detection of changed data without the need for expensive and complex custom

solutions traditionally involving a combination of triggers, timestamp columns, new tables to

store tracking information, and cleanup processes.
