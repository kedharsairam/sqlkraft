The Always On availability groups system policies are used by the Always On Dashboard to

provide information on the availability group health to the user. They are very useful for initial

troubleshooting of an availability group's operational issues. These policies can be extended

and used to customize the Always On Dashboard, or run instantly to report the desired health

information.

There are 14 system policies for availability groups. For detailed information on each policy, see
