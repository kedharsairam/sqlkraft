LDDB_STORAGE_VERIFY

Message Text

Attempting to restore this backup may encounter storage space problems. Subsequent

messages will provide details.

The RESTORE VERIFYONLY statement checks the available storage space on the disk to which

the database is to be restored.

The available disk space may be insufficient to restore the backup being verified.

Restore the backup to a location with sufficient disk space, or provide more space on the disk.
